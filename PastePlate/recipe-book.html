How to Use the Recipe Book

  1. Select Load Recipe Book and choose a CSV created by Paste to Plate.
  2. Your saved recipes will appear below as recipe cards.
  3. Select a recipe card to see its ingredients and complete instructions.
  4. Use the search icon at the bottom-right to find recipes by name, category, ingredients, or directions.
