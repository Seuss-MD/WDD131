Paste to Plate is a recipe card and recipe book converter that takes messy recipe text from a website and displays it cleaned up. You can save it as a CSV file for later use.

Paste your recipe

How to enter a recipe

  1. Paste the ingredient list into the Ingredients box, one ingredient per line.
  2. Paste the preparation steps into the Instructions box.
  3. Enter a title and category if you want; both are optional.
  4. Select Convert Recipe to preview the recipe card.
